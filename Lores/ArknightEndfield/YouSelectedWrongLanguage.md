# You selected wrong language

This special face to face cutscene will play if you are about to choose Voice Language other than default, which is `Original / English`.

First, you will be asked confirmation, then offered options. By your chosen decision, the game will obey your choice anyway. But I think it's cool to tell them in cool ways.

## Are you sure?

DaNeBeau: Hi. This is DaNeBeau speaking. I know, I know, but hear me out. You are currently talking to me through this prerecorded message, so hands of the `Alt` + `F4`, thank you very much.

.

DaNeBeau: You are about to choose language other than our recommended setting. Again, don't be alarmed, this procedure cutscene is played, exclusively to remind you, that you will lose many cool quirks of speaking, **much significantly**.

.

Use KDE's font selector sample text `Kucing sphynx berwajah oval milik Azis tantang Quokka adu fisik`

DaNeBeau: Especially, when an Operator speaks in their native language, such as `Howlerbeast berwajah oval milik Azis tantang Quokka adu fisik`. Or more exciting ones like regional such as, `Kowe turune jem piroh?, Ojo dolan larut mulu!`. Stuff like that.

> Oval faced Howlerbeast belonged to Azis challenges Quokka to brawl

> What time did you sleep? Pls stop playing overnight repeatedly!

.

DaNeBeau: Well, you'll lose all of this, among other cool verbal features once you get out of this default language.

.

DaNeBeau: No big deal, this Save Account is after-all yours, to own, and yours to mend. Just to remind you, that's it.

.

DaNeBeau: So, are you sure to change the language now into, ...

show the pending selected choice

DaNeBeau: ⏪🫴⏩ This? Just select whichever option using either directional arrow button, and then press your confirm button.

Decide now! `Yes` or `No`?

## Cancel

DaNeBeau: Wise choice. Your option remains unchanged.

## Confirm

DaNeBeau: How pitiful. I wish that you know what you're doing.

**Language changed**. DaNeBeau now speaks in selected dub

DaNeBeau: May you one day regret and repent from your falsehood. Please do not forget to return to the righteous way soon. Hehe..

## End
