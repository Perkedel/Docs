# Cofee CCTV

in Doohickey Capsule canteen room, the amenity station has a dedicated high resolution cameras pointing exactly to those amneity doohickeys on the table. This lets Operator visually check each of the doohickey status. Coffee machine, milk dispenser, tea brewer, everything there.

It was inspired from a funny UNIX story, about that coffee pot live feed camera system, `xcoffee`.
