# Ransacker Derby

Endfield & Blue Archive New Paradigm minigame

## Minigame

Kart minigame. You play as Nolok who's just joined thugs. You have maps to ransack

- KDE Valley
- every Kivotos maps
- ZZZ maps
- and newer Endfield maps

## Lore roughly

Blue Archive era

Nolok was bored. He kept being defeated by Tux and friends. He now looks for something new.

Then, a group of thugs encountered Nolok. After friendly discussion, they set an idea to ransack every place in Kivotos

And so they set wheels. Ransacking every facilities around Kivotos and to more maps

Eventually, Nolok got arrested as well as the other rascal Students. The loss was fatally huge, and even caused over 50 businesses went bankrupt. Thankfully, only strong victims like Students, because if it included regular human, there would be gib casualties. He was then thrown into VIP prison of Judgment Realm.

Nolok was locked in a huge cage, inside a marble cube with around square stair which at the center the cage is, and the cube is inside admantium box, secured with every secure locking mechanism unimaginable.

Nolok: You need some enemies? There!, have at it, and stop quarreling yourselves, (accelerate) Mwahahahahahaaa!!
