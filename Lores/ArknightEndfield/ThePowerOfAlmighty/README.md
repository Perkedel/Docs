# The Power of Almighty

Patch Story.

Endmin tries highlights of God's power, each spread through different proxies.

Endmin & DaNeBeau explore different realms and places across Surreal Blender Meme, and each level represents different power of the Almighty.

Inspired from film Bruce Almighty & Evan Almighty. This Story Quest teaches you the amazing advantages of being God, while also teaching you the caveats in every power.
