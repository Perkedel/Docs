## Puzzle Chamber

Here is a mystery portal-like chamber game! The chamber design is same, but there are 3 types adjustable, that you need to solve all.

### Entry & Exit Mechanism

- Pick one of three tags. These are after the said Social Tag. Endmin will wear this.
- Enter the chamber. The first tunnel is the introduction. It tells you interactible story based on the tag type.
- Once you finish intro, move on to the next chamber and the real puzzle begins.
- Solve this puzzle
- Once exit, handover the tag you had to open the door to the tunnel there. You will walk the tunnel that leads you back to the start.
- Now repeat this process for the res 2

### Anxious Type

The Red Tag level type

- Tells you about Repel
- The Operator you control repels some objects during this type.
- There is this Repel Ball & Cube that repels away from you.

### Consent Type

The Yellow Tag level type

- Tells you about doubt and expectancy
- Unstable platform?
- Indiana Jones Trap Floor?
- Defuse with timer??

### Cuddle Type

The Green Tag level type

- Tells you about Cuddling.
- There is a doll that hugs you when being carried. Unlike typical Weighted Storage Cube, Fluffy Cuddle Doll has different pose animation when being dragged, which is hugging you.
- Also, the doll activates certain level elements when being carried, and stops when dropped. This is also the design of the puzzle.
- The doll in the intro wants to hug you. Go ahead carry the doll. HAHA there, it hugs you, but also it activates the door into the real puzzle.
- Yep, carry this doll with you, and this doll is one of the Cube used in the puzzle.
- The dolls can hug each other too! You can carry both when bound. You can also unbind them. Use this to your advantage such as permanently activating something by the doll (either or both).
