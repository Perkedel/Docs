## DNB Finance Feature: Giro

Most business send command paper compatible to other banks just in case the clients are indeed. This is called Cheque. If you cannot do digital transfer now, and the wiring is exclusively intended in-banks-to-banks only, you can create this transfer order form like so.

I never heard anyone would generally use Giro on the other hand. Giro afaik is also command form, but only works in the same bank. Both businesses and client must be the same bank, so cheque is more popular and giro is dead.

But current Cheque system since agnostic can create security risk. Cheque does not have field recipient unlike Giro. Anyone who had cheque can liquify the amount written to their own bank accounts.
