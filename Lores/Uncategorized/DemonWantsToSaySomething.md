## Meta

This is a special model of prison. Each inmate are locked inside a strong glass cage, which is covered by an admantium steel. The cover can be opened anytime to reveal the inmate inside, and works like culinary hemisphere dome. This hood cover is very heavy, so there is a mechatronic to pull the hood up, and when it close, the mechatronic just drops it causing Bam thunk sound. The model is sprinkled with magic so to cancel vibration and any dangerous physical reality effect

And these cages are carried in a conveyor system, like sushi restaurant. A warden manages the placement of the cage, and bring some close to them, e.g., if an inmate of that cage there requested chat, or questionings.
