# Emerald Happy Birthday

Emerald Happy Birthday is an interstitial reward popup that appears everytime you've logged in after your birthday elapsing.

## Mechanism

- Using song Jamrud - Selamat Ulang Tahun
- Rhythmically do this presentation, [here lore](Lores/MultiTalent/CongratulationForStayingAlive.md).
- Rewards you resources and exclusive birthday themed item of the year
- And message you about motivation to stay alive and avoid suicide, because
  - Your heart already beats this number of times
  - Since this date of your Pinball Agung Swipe Card issue, you have this amount of resources collected
  - You have collected this numbers of Characters and the weapons and outfits
- Requires your Birthdate (Only Date & Month) to be set correctly.
  - If your Pinball Agung Swipe Card here linked to your KTP DNB, it'll fetch your birth date & month automatically
