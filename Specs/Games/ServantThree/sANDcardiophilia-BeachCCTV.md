# Beach CCTV

Mini game of Nihil Lease in s&Cardiophilia.

## Rough

- You are actually CCTVing a beach
- Steal This game: `Cleaner: Trash to Treasure` by Stello124; Then break it apart
  - Looks like the trashing part had to be removed, because here in DNB, people are High SDM!
- Because it's live, you do not buy visitors. Instead, you buy events to hold.
- Buy numbers of events!
  - First unlock spawns one somewhere
  - Buy again to Upgrade power
  - More powerful an event attracts more visitor
- As the consequences, every visitor loaded in your gameplay scene counts every heartbeat wow!
- Some of the visitors even stay night to night
- But at the midnight of IRL sunday, the beach is cleared & closed, and all event you bought will be reset back to 0. **NIHILISM REBIRTH**
  - Reopens after IRL 6 AM, whatever considered server day change.
- Lore
  - This endeavour is an experiment
  - What it feels to have this premise compactedly crowded. Will in infuriate the demons around?
  - A question of full spacing. Instruction to spread and conquer the world as in Genesis.
