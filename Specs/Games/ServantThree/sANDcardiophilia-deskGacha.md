# Desk Gacha

Inspire this gacha menu from Apperture Desk Job.

## rough

- The menu while similar with all Gacha games, takes skeleton from Arknights: Endfield
- This time, you sit on a table.
- Bottom right corner is mode switch. It's a blender radio button, 2 modes
  - 1x
  - 10x / Max
- Once a button is selected, start by pulling the lever. You'll see the slider on the right side of screen. For best experience, use Dualsense. the R2 will adaptive trigger harden.
- And once pulled full, the machine will spew as many as hollow dice as the mode selected.
- Each dice once landed, has a sphere that rolls what you get for more seconds.
- The result goes from a 4⭐ to 6⭐. 
- New items gives an explosion, progressively getting more spectacular by tier of luxuryness, And each NEW 6⭐ WIN 50/50 will have the most spectacular explosion.
- You can adjust how the *come in* cutscene plays.
  - New Only (default). Automatically skips dupes and only play new items hired queue you never had yet.
  - Always play. Play all of the hired queue cutscenes no matter what. Great for Broadcasting to all enjoyers. Though, you can always replay anything you had in Character archive menu.
- Theme this ala Apperture Desk Job, where mechanical parts of the buttons and levers are part of the desk, but remember to make this Frutiger Aero, because We are in Encrypten era.
