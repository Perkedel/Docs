# Muni

a Van Elektronische division pertaining to Audio, especially live stage audio.

It's basically Open Source knock off of NEXO. We got all-distance stage speakers too! Instead of Horeg, Muni lets you hear in almost 1 to 1 clarity no matter where you sit or stand at, zero earrape, zero distortion.

Muni in Javanese means `sound`. and Rungu means `hear`.
