# Needles Racing Team gesture

The Needles racing team yell-yell gesture imitates the Needles brand logo, which is `stabbing heart organ with electric needles`.

A pair of optical fiber wired glowsticks are provided. Hold both sticks where the are on the top, stretch, and play gesture where you *stab* the bottom of the stick to your chest. Hold the switch on one of the sticks to start flashing the LED wire connecting the sticks.

Use this as a yell-yell for a victory event.
