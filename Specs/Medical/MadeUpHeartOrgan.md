# Made up heart organ

A spare beating heart meat organ that is manufactured. No more donor needed, just 3D print one and use it.

## Reliability

Made up Heart Organ is built to be even more reliable. When built-in heart can fail, surgical replacement into this allows restored life expectancy of those individuals in need.

Reduced chance of errors and fails, stronger coronary & improved recyclement

## Not for voluntary

Made up heart organ surgery installation is designed for emergency case only, **not for replacing already working heart**. Because there are alot of extra damage factors always happening with surgery, no matter how perfect you guys are. As ones said, **If ain't broke, don't fix it**.
