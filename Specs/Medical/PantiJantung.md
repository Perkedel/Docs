Cardio House is a collective house designed for patients with heart cases, sometimes also not with any heart case, undetectable suspicions, and other research kinds. It's an Orphanage, but for heart case, disease or not.

A group of Medical Doohickers found that the typical vibes of hospital has been cursed since long ago. Not to mention of Horror rides & entertainment businesses often abused Hospital vibes commercially for sake of fear, such as *Srynge is Scary & can kill you*, stuff like that. As the result, many people chose to suffer and die eventually, than have to feel agonizing vibe and eventually injections which is the main scare point for many. Despite many rumor breakers including *Not always injections*, the scare still lingers to this day.

Inspired from Crossban's old project of House of Peace, DNB once again reinstated the program under a different purpose, that is to hopefully attempt curbing horrorness of anything that's medical. They ran few tests on select small towns across Realms. Followed by bigger cities as well. Unfortunately, it's failed because city people (who were the supposed target cure) still had fear of medical stuffs, other stuffs like Orphanage abusive parents cases, and DNB forcedly has to once again cancel this idea.
