# Real Hardware or Emulation?

Do you prefer experience on **`Real Hardware`**, or **`Emulation / FPGA`**?

## Pros & Cons

- Real
  - Pros
    - It's real itself
    - Bug2Bug of itself
  - Cons
    - Ever aging & maintenance needed
    - Worn out
    - Indeed buggier the age goes
    - Uses the physical space
- Emu-FPGA
  - Pros
    - No more concern of physical deteoriation
    - Never gone, in theory. Just need casings images, ROMs, Digitized Chips, and you're good to go
    - Only space needed is your disk left.
    - Put billions of hardwares in just 1 tiny disk.
  - Cons
    - Heavy on performance
    - Even FPGA may lose accuracy
