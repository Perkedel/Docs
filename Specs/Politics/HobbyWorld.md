# Hobby World

A badge awarded to a region up to whole planet for housing over 100 unique RabbitHoles / Hobbies within its measurement scale.

## Implementation

Hobby World award was established by a group ancient Surreal beings since 1950. The people there was seeking unique and fun entertainment where their home world is quite empty and too abstract. So abstract it's mundane for them.

Hobby World expects that each measured RabbitHole has clear purpose and mechanics, i.e. not abstract, and considerably playable as product with obvious concepts. It also expect that every dweller maintains their holes for a very long time, and not just being a new hobby.

A contestant can only win Hobby World of the Year once in a timeline. Losing and new contestant and join next contest and have chance to win.

## Boredom

The only winner was that Parim's older planet. Alas, no other planet or country interested for being the next. So Hobby World eventually goes forgotten as and never been heard anymore by 2009.

## Drama

As being too flexed on the winning, people began to forget who they strive for, to help others find worthy and fun life. Since 2020, chief dwellers starts getting arrogang and over-expecting against other dwellers, Resulting civil wars and RabbitHole deaths.  
Notably including Parim from Cardiophilia, Vacation Island's senators, and many more.

## Too late

New Hobby pop-ups only strikes back ever since DNB was established for the first time in 2033. But the ancient Surreal beings have since retired and gone into long resting mode, which likely won't wake up until Jesus coming back.
