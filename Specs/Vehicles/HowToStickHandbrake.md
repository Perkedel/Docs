# How to use Stick Handbrake

For Racetrack and other legacy cars

## Disengage

1. Push and hold button good pressure
2. **Pull up**
3. Push down

## Re-engage

1. Pull it real good until peaks

## Wtf?!

- Stick handbrake works like Ratchets. Keep this in mind
- Reason why you also pull up to disengage is because of this exact special mechanism. to really help pull ratchet pin off from the tooth.
