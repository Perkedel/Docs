# Seals of Revelation

A theme series based on Bible final book of New Testament, Revelation, Ch. 6.

The cars here are colored based on those 6 seals appeared in the book, such as

- White. with Arrow, Bow, & Quill
- Maroon. with great sword of peace
- Black. with Scale of Justice, gold accents
- Green Pale. With scythe of Death, not to be confused with Chicks from Cars.
- Purple. it's a coffin carrier car, *carrying allegedly down Overseer inside a coffin there*.
- Blue. the `Apocalypse` *where the planet trembles & the sun dies & dwarfs*.
