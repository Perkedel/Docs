# Stork Sikil & Swiwi

Sikil (Foot) & Swiwi (Wing) are series for Motoring system on drones.  
Sikil are motorings designed for Surface. Wheeled motors and stuffs.  
While Swiwi are propelled motoring systems. Bladed, jets, and stuffs.

Yes, Javanese name again. It's once again a tribute to my mom's root.
