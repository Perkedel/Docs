# Tasted Rice

## The Difference in Step

1. Keep rice fully washed. Basically when rinsed, all white dust in it no longer found
2. **Dry first, but not till it's back to bone**
  - You may sprinkle salt to the rice
  - dry with Dehydrator box 
3. **Instead of adding regular drinking water base, use flavoured soup base**. You can have
  - Chicken stew
  - Mushroom stew
  - Mega-Truffle stew
  - Sinkdown Seafood vaganza. lol Susi Pudjiastuti's `Tenggelamkan`
