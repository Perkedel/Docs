# Escape Terrorism

Premise suddenly got attacks?

## Condition

- DNB lets you carry weapons. real weapons of any kinds, including witchcraft
- A group of Harassers suddenly raided the place

## How to escape

- **Just run & escape**. 
- **Do not engage**. You did not analyze what are those Harassers are capable of. Assume they had LVL Infinity than you are.
- Keep your weapons ready & loaded. 
- Your weapons shall exclusively be used **if you had to**
- Lift your shield and block the bullets & Harasser's source of DMG
- Don't get spotted
- Escape as far as you can, best if you stopped by police / security office.
- Collect as many as other visitors of the place, until your vehicle critically can't load anymore. Then run as fast and as far as you can.
- Always accept plea to hitch your ride at all costs, better save lives than ignore.
- Just escape far, then call police.
- Do not let Harasser hear you reporting!
- Stuck in confined space? Mute yourself!
  - Shut your mouth, never accidentally talk!
  - Never accidentally stumbles
  - If necessary, decrease your heartbeat volume as much as you can. You cannot stop it tho, you need to live.
