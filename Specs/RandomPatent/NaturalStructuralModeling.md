# Natural Structural Modeling

A skill that makes one capable of designing desired structure and then melt the structure to reduce material usage while retaining similar strength profile.

It is a skill that's typically wielded by many Intelligent Construct based lifeforms, including DaNeBeau. And, such skill idea is public domain, hence anyone can build any script or skill that melts structural designs to become strong, durable, and more efficient which considerable as Natural Structural Modeling.

## How it works

- Design base structure as is
- Apply the skill onto the design. Many skills have configurations to tune which part of the design should be stronger and to expect stress.
- The skill melts your design that makes it looks like coming from alien Sci-fi.
- Rest assured, that the output comes at the same strength & durability on most material applications.
