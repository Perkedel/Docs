# As long it works

a production discipline that rushes things up. as long as the quality is imperceptible against markets, it will be considered valid

e.g., Video production is complicated. So you just record whatever, slap it in the reel, and arrange it whatever. You can see one shot has LOG this and LUT that, and another LOG another and LUT other. Color becomes inconsistent, even tho it's too slight to be noticable, but then most audience didn't even bother, coz your story is way too good.
